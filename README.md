2. Sales Overview

This page gives the high-level business summary.

Slicers: Year, Month, Country and Product.
KPI cards: Total Sales (114.76M), Number of Orders (200K), Boxes Shipped (27M) and Average Sale per Box (4.18).
Total Sales by Month and Year: an area chart comparing 2022 and 2023. Sales are about 4.5M–5M for most of the year, dip around June, and rise sharply in November and December to about 5.5M. This shows strong seasonality, with a year-end peak.
Boxes Shipped and Total Sales by Product: a donut chart. The largest product shares are 31.96% and 29.12%.
Total Sales by Quarter and Year: a clustered column chart. Q1 to Q3 are about 14M each, and Q4 is the highest at about 15M. 2022 and 2023 are very close.
Top 5 Monthly details: a table of Total Sales and Boxes Shipped. November and December are among the top months, which matches the seasonal trend.

3. Product & Country

This page compares product and country performance.

Slicers: Year, Country and Product.
KPI cards: Top Product (Mixed Assortment Box), Top Country (USA), Products Sold (13) and Sales per Country (14.35M).
Total Sales by Product: a horizontal bar chart. Mixed Assortment Box leads with 14.9M, followed by Pralines Gift Box (13.4M) and Truffle Gift Box (13.2M). The rest are between about 8.0M and 8.9M.
Total Sales by Country and Product: a stacked column chart for USA, Germany, UK, Vietnam and Australia. USA is the tallest, and the product mix is similar across countries.
Average Sale per Box by Product: a column chart. The premium gift boxes have the highest value per box (Mixed Assortment about 17, Pralines 13, Truffle 11), while bars and the other products are only about 3–4.
Total Sales by Country: a donut chart of seven countries. Shares range from about 13.2% to 16.4%, so sales are spread fairly evenly. USA is highest at 16.42%.

4. Channel & Salesperson

This page covers sales channels and individual salesperson performance.

Slicers: Year, Salesperson, Channel and Country.
KPI cards: Top Channel (Wholesale), Top Salesperson (SP_010), Boxes per Salesperson (1.06M) and Sales per Salesperson (4.41M).
Total Sales by Salesperson: a horizontal bar chart of the top 10. SP_010 leads with 4.9M, and the others are close together at about 4.6M–4.7M.
Total Sales by Month and Channel: a stacked column chart of Online, Retail and Wholesale. Monthly totals are fairly steady and rise in November and December.
Total Sales by Channel: a donut chart. Wholesale is 37.42%, Online 33.08% and Retail 29.51%.
Boxes Shipped vs Total Sales by Salesperson: a scatter plot. Sales rise with boxes shipped, and SP_010 is a clear outlier at the top right.
